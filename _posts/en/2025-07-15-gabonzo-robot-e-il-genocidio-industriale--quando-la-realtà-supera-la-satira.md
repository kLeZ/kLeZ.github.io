*A Nanowar of Steel song becomes the perfect metaphor for understanding what is happening in Gaza, and why I can't sleep at night any more.*

A few days ago I was listening to "Gabonzo Robot" by Nanowar of Steel. For those who don't know them, they're Italian geniuses of parody metal who have turned irony into a musical art form. The song, from the album "Italian Folk Metal", is a power metal anthem dedicated to a robot, the hero of an imaginary anime series. Nothing special, if it weren't for one line the band uses to describe their "hero": **"The only superhero that always wins because he fights against the weak and the children"**.

I laughed. Then I stopped laughing.

Because that line, said as a joke about an imaginary robot fighting non-existent enemies, describes with surgical precision what has been happening in Gaza for more than **a year and nine months**. And there's nothing left to laugh about.

Gabonzo Robot is the invincible one who only wins against the weak and the children. Israel is the fifth-largest military power in the world, exterminating a population that has been under siege for 18 years, killing **17,492 confirmed children**. 

Reality has outdone satire, and this article is my desperate attempt to process a situation that is costing me my sleep and my faith in humanity.

### When Numbers Stop Being Numbers

Before carrying on with the robot metaphors, let's do the maths. The real maths. The kind that should have all of us on our feet screaming, but that instead we read in the papers between a washing powder advert and a piece on the new iPhone.

**Since 7 October 2023:**
- **[62,614 Palestinians dead](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/longform/2023/10/9/israel-hamas-war-in-maps-and-charts-live-tracker)**. Think about that number. It's more than the population of Piacenza. Wiped out.
- **[17,492 children killed](https://www.savethechildren.org/us/about-us/media-and-news/2024-press-releases/gaza-ten-thousand-killed-in-nearly-100-days-of-war)**. Seventeen *thousand*. Not 17, not 170. **Seventeen *thousand***.
- **[125,000 wounded](https://www.unrwa.org/resources/reports/unrwa-situation-report-165-situation-gaza-strip-and-west-bank-including-east-jerusalem)**. If it were an Italian city, it would be the size of Reggio Emilia. Every single one wounded.
- **[1.9 million displaced](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/longform/2023/10/9/israel-hamas-war-in-maps-and-charts-live-tracker)** out of 2.3 million inhabitants. 90% of the population.

These aren't war numbers. They're numbers of **systematic extermination**.

And on the other side? **[1,139 Israelis dead](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20241007-hamas-terrorist-attacks-7-october-deadliest-day-israel-history-anniversary)** in the 7 October attack. Every human life matters, every death is a tragedy, but the ratio is **1 to 55**. One to fifty-five. It's as if for every person who hit you, you killed another 54. And kept going. For a year and three months.

What do you call that, if not genocide?

### The documented genocide

Now that we've identified the directors, let's look at what they've produced. And here things get really grim, because what is happening in Gaza is not "war". It's **industrial genocide**, documented in real time by the most respected international organisations in the world.

**[Human Rights Watch](https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/03/19/israeli-forces-conduct-gaza)** - March 2024: "Crimes against humanity and acts of genocide". They analysed 280+ videos and gathered 212 first-hand testimonies.

**[Amnesty International](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/mde15/8668/2024/en/)** - December 2024: "You Feel Like You Are Subhuman" - a 300-page report that concludes: **Israel is committing genocide**. They analysed over 100 statements by Israeli officials showing "genocidal intent".

**[Médecins Sans Frontières](https://msf.org.uk/issues/gaza-genocide)**: "What our teams have witnessed is consistent with descriptions of genocide". MSF, which has been working in wars for 50 years, says Gaza is different. It's worse.

And the **[International Court of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_genocide)**? On 26 January 2024 it ruled that the genocide allegations are **"plausible"** and ordered Israel to stop. Israel responded by stepping up the bombing.

When the three most respected human rights organisations in the world, together with the International Court of Justice, all say the same thing, maybe it's worth listening.

But the person who has documented all of this with the greatest precision is **[Francesca Albanese](https://www.ohchr.org/en/special-procedures/sr-palestine/francesca-albanese)**, UN Special Rapporteur on human rights in the Palestinian territories. Her reports are a masterpiece of documentation and a punch in the gut for anyone who still has a shred of conscience.

**["Anatomy of a Genocide"](https://news.un.org/en/story/2024/03/1147976)** (March 2024): Albanese identifies three genocidal acts under the 1948 Convention:
1. **Killing members of the group**
2. **Causing serious bodily or mental harm** 
3. **Inflicting conditions of life calculated to bring about physical destruction**

All three are documented in Gaza. Systematically. Daily.

**["From Economy of Occupation to Economy of Genocide"](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/7/1/un-report-lists-companies-complicit-in-israels-genocide-who-are-they)** (June 2025): The most devastating report. Albanese documents how **48 international corporations** (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon) are profiting from the extermination. The **Tel Aviv Stock Exchange rose by 213%** during the genocide.

Yes, you read that right. They're making money off genocide. Gabonzo Robot at least didn't invoice for children's corpses.

The response? The **[United States imposed sanctions on Albanese](https://apnews.com/article/francesca-albanese-gaza-genocide-sanctions-un-israel-ff0501f318b7dd0d923c30f10b639724)**. Not on those committing genocide, but on the person documenting it. It's like sanctioning the sports commentator instead of the team that's doping.
